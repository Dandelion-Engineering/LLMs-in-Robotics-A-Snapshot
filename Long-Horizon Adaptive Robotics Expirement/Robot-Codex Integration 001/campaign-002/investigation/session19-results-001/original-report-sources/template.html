What the fixed allocation actually produced

95% Wilson intervals are descriptive summaries of valid attempts under an independence assumption. Model-service independence is unproven. Infrastructure-based selection and the early stop mean these intervals are not a calibrated guarantee of campaign reliability. Pooled valid-trial estimates follow the actual valid counts, which may differ by fixture. All-planned bounds retain the fixed equal-fixture allocation. This campaign has two known challenge fixtures, not 20 independent task families. Unresolved slots stay visible in all-planned bounds.

How to read the denominators and bounds

Valid success / valid assessed attempts excludes infrastructure-invalid evidence. All-planned lower and upper bounds divide by every scheduled slot: confirmed valid successes form the lower bound; treating every unresolved or unrun slot as successful forms the upper bound. Those bounds are sensitivity limits, not confidence intervals or claims that unrun work succeeded.

Task failure with intact infrastructure remains a valid failure. A successful queue receipt authenticates execution, not the robot's task outcome. Observed evaluator completion is a separate diagnostic count from authenticated assessments, including infrastructure-invalid attempts. It can show task completion despite a timing failure, but never changes scored successes or Wilson denominators. Started denominators require a recorded official queue start timestamp; absent assessments contribute no observed outcome.
