## Prospective dev005 infrastructure only

Every response is journaled and fsynced before forwarding, then receives a delivery marker only after send returns. Each record has connection identity, sequence, previous digest, and canonical-record digest. Output Base64 is decoded for capability redaction; original and retained byte hashes/counts are separate. Redacted streams cannot establish an unchanged source read.

The hard bound is **64 MiB of serialized capture journals combined across all executor connections of one invocation**, including request/response metadata and delivery records. It is not a per-message or model-output allowance. Crossing the bound or failing to persist capture sets the abort event and stops forwarding; no silent truncation is accepted. Existing stderr/request evidence remains separately retained and subject to the campaign's overall storage monitor. A capture without a final closed receipt cannot pass. Cleanup/retirement still runs on capture failure.
