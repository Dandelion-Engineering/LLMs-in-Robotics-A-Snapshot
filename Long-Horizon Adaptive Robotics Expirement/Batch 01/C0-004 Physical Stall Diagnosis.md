The retained [RGB capture 419](../Evidence/raw/B01-dev-C0-004/frames/000419.png) visibly contains the white robot over the upper stock region. The original image-only detector sampled a 7-by-5-pixel crop at a projected 18 mm horizontal offset from the colored tag and classified mean RGB above 0.72 as processed. It did not check crop depth or occlusion. Reconstruction from that recorded RGB/depth pair and public camera/tag calibration exactly matches B10's 83 tag pixels. The tag median is `(u=269, v=280)` at depth **1.214199 m**. The mark crop is centered at `(277,280)`, has mean RGB **0.998693**, but median depth **0.649791 m**: approximately **564 mm in front of the tag**. None of its 35 pixels is within 4 mm of the tag depth. The crop samples the occluding white robot, rather than a visible B10 mark. This is a directly measured false positive, not merely an inferred possibility involving threshold noise or tag color.

Following parent authorization, the common detector was corrected before the next physical calibration freeze. [sensor_worker.py](../Runtime/sensor_worker.py) now retains the colored-tag detection but reports an unknown mark unless at least 90% of a complete crop jointly supports the same depth plane and an unambiguous appearance. Depth agreement is within 4 mm. Processed pixels require all RGB channels above 0.72 and channel spread no greater than 0.10; raw pixels require maximum channel below 0.25. Other appearances, clipped crops, nonfinite measurements, or occlusions yield `visible_mark=unknown`. Inputs remain normalized RGB, depth and the observed tag depth. No body pose, evaluator truth, cycle history or hidden label enters classification. Existing candidate code already treats unknown marks as lacking raw/processed evidence.

Eight focused mark tests cover normal raw/processed surfaces, the measured B10 depth counterexample, dark occlusion, background, gray/colored/mixed appearances, incomplete/nonfinite input, joint depth-and-color support, detector integration, and ±0.5 mm depth/±1 DN color offsets. Together with existing sensor/candidate checks, **64 tests passed in 1.250 s**. A stale sensor-worker setup test was updated to provide the old insert's returned-home observation and open jaws required by the previously applied insert-exchange correction; its post-motion fresh-capture assertion remains. No physics, rendering, model inference or queue job ran for this diagnosis/correction.
