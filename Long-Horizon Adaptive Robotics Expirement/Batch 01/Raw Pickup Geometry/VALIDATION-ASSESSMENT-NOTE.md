# Mechanical assessment still needed after construction

Read-only pipeline audit; this note chooses no cells, limits, margins, trials or qualification outcome. Root selects the provisional numerical rule from construction before the original validation worlds run.

## Present capability

`run_screen.py --phase validation` requires a prior destination-matched provisional artifact and copies/hash-pins it before launch. The completed-screen extractor checks the successful screen receipt, selected/deferred trial coverage, consumed raw artifact bytes and exact copied rule identity/order. It preserves all declared outcomes, rich states, compiled parameter points, full cut-to-admission hold accounting, and dense native-tick geometry unions.

`bundle_construction.validate_provisional_rule` checks structure and identity, explicitly **not numerical or physical support**. Runtime's `_grip` and `_route` implement the actual numerical start-state checks after qualification identity passes. The protected loader verifies artifact bytes, map/cell identity and declared support labels; it does not inspect a validation trajectory or derive the truth of those support labels. `run_screen` records the proposed rule but does **not** apply its cells to trial results.

## Smallest missing component

Add one separate, read-only **validation assessor** consuming the exact root-selected rule, completed construction/validation extractions and independently supplied successful receipts for their queued execution/extraction. It should emit per-trial, per-check pass/fail/unknown with exact source references, preserving failed/missed/unstarted trials. It must not refit cells, choose alternate cells, expand corridors, add padding, change thresholds, create trials or grant qualification.

The assessor should reuse Runtime's numerical semantics while keeping identity approval and final qualification separate. For calibration support, a claimed valid measured hold window must be **at least** the root-declared prospective duration; later episode coverage instead asks whether its actual absence is **at most** that supported duration. These are distinct comparisons. Synthetic checks should cover a state outside one bound, a dense box outside the fixed corridor union, a wrong mapped cell, transient hold failure, wrong/missing admission endpoint, source/event mismatch, missing validation trial and an altered post-validation rule. These are counterexamples to the declared rule, not new physical trials or thresholds.

## Measurement limits the assessor cannot repair

The every-tick hold accumulator records the unchanged **hand contact + center height >75 mm** predicate and height minima. It does not record every-tick bilateral force/relative-pose/velocity envelopes. Bilateral force is available at the rich landmarks and ordinary contact trace samples; dense geometry does not turn those into every-tick contact evidence. Therefore an assessor can verify the existing discrete hold claim and force membership at recorded states, but cannot certify continuous/every-tick bilateral envelope membership from absent data. Other-object/contact facts are likewise sampled, despite dense arm/payload outer boxes. A stronger rule remains unsupported rather than being inferred from final placement.

Numerical `route_sampling_clearance_allowance_m` and declared `supports` labels are presently shape-checked, not matched to measured geometry/support by the loader. The missing assessor supplies that evidence-to-rule link; it must keep observed discrete sampling deficit distinct from any root-frozen empirical-transfer padding or continuous-motion claim.

`bundle_construction.py` previously attempted to turn the extractor's `recovery_admission` action/response event into a snapshot, which lacks `snapshot_sha256`. It now processes exactly the six rich snapshot landmarks, retains the admission event separately with payload-digest verification and exact timing/source reference, and preserves other records. An actual-shaped synthetic regression covers all six landmarks, admission tampering and a damaged rich snapshot. **Five bundle-construction tests passed.** Only that helper and its delegated test were edited; no fixture or Runtime source changed.
