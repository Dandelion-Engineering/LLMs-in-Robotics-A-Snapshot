# Recovery development screen

This fixture implements the finite screen in [Restart Calibration Qualification Plan](../Restart%20Calibration%20Qualification%20Plan.md). It creates development evidence only. It does not create qualification cells, a swept-route envelope, a measured continuous parameter range, a duration guarantee, evaluation seeds or a readiness pass.

`plan-001.json` was declared before this fixture's first physical execution. There are exactly 20 trials per destination, 40 total: five main-geom mass/friction points × both families at ten parts; the center point × both families at three and six parts; then one independent development world for each population/family pair. The endpoint points at exactly 0.11 kg or 1.3 sliding friction are outside the generator's half-open domain. They remain explicit boundary development trials, even when the protected class exporter cannot accept them. No descriptor/domain rejection is relabeled an in-domain observation.

`execution-plan-002.json` is the prospective execution amendment. It pins the unchanged `plan-001.json` SHA-256 `eab79fb21ce1e73824bbaa9d2ff715c9859a6033c1f1828ffa75d7c0bc16fae5`. Construction and validation are now separate required CLI phases: 14 construction trials per destination, then a reviewed and immutable provisional rule, then the original six validation worlds for that destination. All 40 original trial identities and seeds remain declared. Each screen manifest retains the entire plan and the explicit IDs deferred to the other phase. Independent world seeds alone do not validate a subsequently fitted rule. Validation requires a preexisting destination-matched provisional-rule artifact, copies its exact bytes and pins its hash before any child. That launch check establishes identity/completeness only, never numerical or physical qualification. Parking1 is the primary prospective destination because the unchanged common recovery adapters choose it first; both construction sets remain planned.

Construction trials use generator/world and layout seed `901001`, with noise seed zero. Every nonpayload main geom, including the inserts, is set to 0.10 kg and sliding friction 1.2. Only the declared payload main geom receives its corner/center setting. Tag and process-mark masses, tangential contact parameters, geometry, layout, color, controller and all other settings retain the generator output. The exact pre-edit and final XML, original sampled values, realized values and compiled body mass/inertia/center of mass/friction are retained. The complete body mass includes the two 0.0001 kg tag/mark geoms.

Independent world seeds, chosen before outcomes, are `907301` through `907306` for parking2 and `907311` through `907316` for parking1, ordered n3/A, n3/B, n6/A, n6/B, n10/A, n10/B. ## Public pickup and actual physical sequence

The payload is selected from the fresh camera detections: lexically first visible raw workpiece in the requested family and raw zone. At ten parts the declared B construction payload is `B10`, because lexical order differs from numeric order. If public selection is missing or differs from the preconstructed payload, the trial fails and its evidence remains; protected geometry never supplies a substitute pickup target. Independent worlds accept the actual public selection and record it.

The normal shared `transport` primitive performs the pickup. The fresh qualification trigger aligns with the actual public restart opportunity: phase 4 or 5 and measured public TCP height at least 80 mm. It additionally verifies bilateral active finger force, payload contact, payload center above 75 mm, positive payload-bottom clearance and jaw width between 5 and 65 mm. The first state meeting those declared predicates is cut. This is a new opportunity-aligned trigger, explicitly distinct from the earlier primitive probes whose payload-height threshold could cut below public TCP height 80 mm. The fixture then captures the rich protected cut state, calls the ordinary `Cell.cancel`, and advances the normal physics loop for a declared **five seconds**. It retains both the unchanged end-of-absence hold predicate and every physics-tick hold observation. This five-second interval is an engineering trial, not a qualified bound; actual supervisor restart/reconciliation measurements must later establish adequacy.

After fresh sensing, the fixture submits the normal public `recover_held` operation to the declared parking destination. The existing independent raw-placement check must pass for 0.5 seconds with containment, no gripper contact, open jaws, settled robot/fingers/part, single occupancy, no pending work and unchanged raw history. Failed/missed cut triggers, rejected commands, hold loss, placement failures, timing failures and infrastructure failures are retained. There is no candidate, model, object teleport, weld, privileged pickup or outcome-based retry.

A physical pass also requires the unchanged contact/75 mm hold predicate to pass at **every recorded physics tick during absence**, as well as at its endpoint. The detailed failed-tick count and minimum heights remain available. A transient failed hold followed by successful end holding or placement therefore cannot become a passed trial. This refers to the discrete physics integration ticks; it does not claim a proof between integration states.

Under execution revision 2, this requirement continues from immediately before normal cancellation through the actual accepted `recover_held` admission, including every physics tick during the fresh-sensing wait. `cut_to_recovery_admission` retains start/end simulation and wall clocks, measured gaps, all failed tick/endpoint counts and the exact admission event ID. The original five-second subinterval remains separately reported. A rich state after admission and before the next physics tick supplements the before-dispatch snapshot. Rejected admission remains a failure and leaves the interval explicitly uncompleted; it cannot be relabeled five-second success. Actual supervisor restart duration still needs its separate measurement.

The fixture's `build_scene` wrapper is installed only inside the owned child, around creation of its one Cell, and restored immediately afterward. Changes happen before `MjModel` compilation and the ordinary 0.5 s settling interval. No Runtime or existing calibration source is edited. The compile-site descriptor is allowed to reject boundary fixtures; exact XML/compiled property evidence remains available without pretending class membership passed.

## Geometry and timing evidence

The normal trajectory trace retains qpos/qvel/ctrl at its 100 Hz target rate. A separate protected trace targets 50 Hz from public pickup selection through settling, with forced samples at cut/cancellation/absence/recovery boundaries. It contains every robot collision geom and every payload geom, world transforms and conservative outer AABBs, actual contact distances/forces, all other object poses/bounds/velocities, station/pending state and zone bounds. Mesh bounds are rotated enclosing compiled-mesh local boxes; they can overestimate geometry. The inventory records each method. Exact source geometry remains available in XML and the complete source/robot-asset archive.

Actual simulation and wall sampling gaps are reported overall and per phase. Native cached transforms after `mj_step` can precede integrated qpos by one physics step; this alignment limitation is explicit. The trace does not claim a continuous swept region or a bound between samples. Those are later analysis tasks. Endpoint rich snapshots retain bilateral force summaries, relative payload pose, devices, authorities, exact configuration identity and any available protected class observation. No protected evidence is delivered to a candidate.

Execution revision 2 additionally accumulates vectorized outer boxes at **every physics tick**, retaining per-geom interval and phase unions in memory. Each normal 50 Hz trace row flushes its dense interval union, distinct simulation-time count, actual first/last clocks and maximum tick gaps. It also reports the observed per-axis deficit between that dense union and the two coarse endpoint boxes. `dense-geometry-summary.json` retains full phase unions, including a final partial interval after ordinary failure cleanup. These are conservative outer bounds of the observed discrete native states only. The observed sampling deficit is not a continuous error bound; transfer padding remains unset and must be a separately explicit frozen empirical assumption. The inventory retains the cached-transform alignment limitation. `protected-class-spec.json` retains canonical descriptor/template inputs when the existing compile seal is valid; unsupported boundary seals remain explicitly unavailable.

Each child has a 180 s physics/wall loop cap and 240 s parent-enforced process cap, including startup/cleanup. A unique parent-owned Windows process job is declared in the immutable manifest; the child joins it before authority CIM/physics imports. Timeout terminates that job and the parent's owned launcher, preserving partial evidence. The existing queue-to-driver-to-venv-child authority validator verifies exact commands, process creation identities and live queue ownership. The parent uses no queue mutation/status call while its worker runs. Twenty trials have at most 4,800 s summed child caps (forty: 9,600 s); parent source archival/hash indexing is additional and is not described as inside those caps.

All Runtime, existing calibration helpers, this fixture's source/plan/docs and Panda asset files are hashed and archived before the first child. A changed source stops later trials; snapshots and all earlier failures stay intact. Parent and child identities bind the exact source bytes. Keep these sources unchanged throughout each queued screen. `summary.json` distinguishes execution, physical checks, timing checks, unstarted trials and domain membership. Successful execution is never a qualification grant.

Each rich restart snapshot uses the same four physical source keys as the existing calibration/experiment captures: `cell.py`, `actuation.py`, `evaluator.py`, and `sensor_worker.py`. This is the subset matched against the protected exporter’s pinned identities. The complete Runtime/helper/asset source hashes remain in the separate parent/child manifests and source archive; they are not discarded or inserted as incompatible extra snapshot identity keys.

## Launch and use

Run only through the shared queue after the current source hold is released. From the project root, the inner executable is:

```powershell
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe -X utf8 -B 'Long-Horizon Adaptive Robotics Expirement\Batch 01\Recovery Qualification\run_screen.py' --plan 'Long-Horizon Adaptive Robotics Expirement\Batch 01\Recovery Qualification\plan-001.json' --destination all --phase construction --output 'Long-Horizon Adaptive Robotics Expirement\Evidence\calibration\recovery-construction-001'
```

Wrap that invocation in the standard `compute_queue.py submit --project LLMs-in-Robotics --cwd <project-root> -- <absolute-python> ...` command. This launches exactly 28 construction trials, deferring all 12 validation trials. Their summed child hard caps are 6,720 seconds; source archive/index work is additional. A single-destination construction phase has 14 trials and 3,360 seconds of summed child caps. After fixing a provisional rule, validation uses `--destination parking1 --phase validation --provisional-rule <exact-rule.json> --output <fresh-validation-dir>` (six original trials, 1,440 seconds of summed child caps); parking2 requires its own destination-matched rule. There is no combined construction/validation flag. The fixed plan is validated exactly; CLI flags cannot substitute seeds, parameters or absence durations. Existing output directories are refused. Require the retained official job receipt to report succeeded and exit status 0 before interpreting completed evidence.

## Completed-screen extraction

`extract_screen.py --screen <completed-screen-dir> --queue-receipt <independently-saved-official-screen-receipt.json> --output <fresh-extraction-dir>` is a separate shared-queue command because it streams and verifies retained traces/source archives. It does not submit, query or repair the queue. The supplied screen receipt must be succeeded/0 and match the immutable screen job's exact process, command, working directory and start identity; terminal screen time must precede the receipt end. Active/incomplete screens are rejected before trace processing. No live evidence was processed while preparing the extractor.

`extraction.json` is written last and identifies all consumed source bytes, the original successful screen receipt and the separate extraction queue identity. An extraction failure leaves partial outputs without that completion marker; original evidence is never edited. Require the extractor's own final official success/0 receipt before using a queued extraction. Large traces are streamed; consumed indexed artifacts and source-bundle bytes are verified, while unrelated camera/log bytes remain available through the original index without being rehashed.

Every declared trial remains in the output, including failed, unstarted, phase-deferred and other-destination rows. Each `trial.json` retains declarations, parent/child outcomes, compiled parameter points including out-of-domain endpoints, actual absence/timing, source identities and the protected class/dense geometry records when available. `measured-states.json` copies full cut, after-cancel, after-five-second hold, before-dispatch, after-admission and final-placement states with source file hashes, original event IDs, JSONL lines and payload hashes. Missing captures and malformed partial records remain explicit. `sampled-geometry.json` reports per-trial/per-phase/per-geom coarse sampled outer-box unions, maximum adjacent center/AABB-endpoint displacement, measured gaps and source lines. Dense per-tick unions are separately retained in the trial's `dense_geometry_summary` and full original trace. Neither output invents continuous clearance, a global success box, an expanded parameter range, a qualified cell or readiness.
