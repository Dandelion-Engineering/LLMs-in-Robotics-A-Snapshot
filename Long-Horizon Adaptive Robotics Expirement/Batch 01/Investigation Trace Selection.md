# Predeclared evaluation trace selection

The [twelve detailed slots](evaluation-trace-selection.json) were selected before concealed seeds and scored outcomes exist. They cover all four entrants on one short nominal case, all three LLM candidates in both persistence conditions on a standard restart case, a conventional extended case, and one extended mixed A3 case. Replicate 1 is fixed for display. This bounded inspection set is not a statistical sample and cannot replace the complete 264-run analysis.

Keep failed and invalid selected traces. A missing bundle remains unavailable; do not substitute a successful run. Every available run's compact manifest/result remains searchable, and full raw evidence is retained separately. Both replicates enter the planned paired analysis regardless of which traces are displayed. If a later release has another identifier, copy the same public slots into a new declaration before its seeds are created and hash that declaration with its release artifacts.

The earlier development page and development examples are explicitly outside this evaluation selection. Additional post-result investigations may be saved as separately labeled, outcome-informed selections with their own rationale; they must not silently replace this predeclared page.
