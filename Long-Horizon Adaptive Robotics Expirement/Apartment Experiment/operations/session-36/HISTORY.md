# How the original Maps cup reached the bathroom

This reconstruction reads the original apartment-v2 Maps history through verified checkpoint 22. It does not advance physics or alter either condition. `history001` authenticated 3,927 replay chunks, 498,375 state samples and 4,095 chained events, and indexed 1,404 observable native transcript items and 324 cup-related file versions/snapshots. `history002` reconstructed contacts at every saved sample in five selected transition windows. Times below are original simulation seconds, not wall time.

Contact classifications are derived with `mj_forward` at recorded states, generally about 32 ms apart in these windows. Contacts between saved samples can be missed. Original contact forces and full solver warmstart/actuator history were not recorded, so reconstructed forces are not historical force telemetry. Energy is derived from saved position/velocity and the frozen body model. The separate isolated forward tests directly retained contact force/work, zero applied/passive/actuator cup forces, and numerical warnings; those tests support the physics interpretation but cannot recreate an unobserved intermediate historical action.

The full original replay, native outputs and learned files remain available for later qualitative study. This account uses completed observable agent messages, tool results, physical events and filesystem artifacts; it makes no claim about unlogged reasoning, causal learning benefit, or Baseline-versus-Maps superiority.
